# AI-Job-Impact-Analytics-Project-PostgreSQL-and-Microsoft-Power-Bi

AI Job Impact Analytics
Project Overview
Artificial Intelligence is transforming the global workforce by reshaping job roles, influencing salaries, improving productivity, and accelerating automation across industries. This project analyses the impact of AI on workforce transformation using PostgreSQL for data analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization.
The objective is to uncover business insights related to AI adoption, job replacement, salary growth, productivity changes, automation risk, and remote work trends across multiple industries.
________________________________________
Project Objective
To analyse how Artificial Intelligence is affecting employees, industries, and organizations by answering key business questions such as:
•	Which industries have the highest AI adoption?
•	How does AI impact employee salaries?
•	What is the effect of AI on productivity?
•	Which industries are at the highest risk of automation?
•	How does remote work influence productivity and job satisfaction?
•	What percentage of jobs are being replaced, modified, or unchanged?
________________________________________
Dataset Information
The Dataset was downloaded from Kaggle.com
The dataset contains workforce-related information across various industries and includes following columns:
Employee_ID, Age, Gender, Education_Level, Industry, Job_Role, Years_Experience, AI_Adoption_Level, Automation_Risk, Upskilling_Required, Salary_Before_AI, Salary_After_AI, Job_Status, Work_Hours_Per_Week, Remote_Work, Job_Satisfaction, Productivity_Change_%

Key Insights: -
•	Total Employees = 2,000
•	job replacement rate = 5.30%. This indicates that AI has replaced only a small portion of workforce.
•	Unchanged Jobs = 1,093
•	Modified Jobs = 801
•	Replaced Jobs = 106
•	This above information indicates that AI is transforming existing roles rather than eliminating them.
•	Salary after AI is consistently higher than salary before AI. 
•	Organizations appear willing to pay more for employees capable of working effectively with AI tools.
•	Productivity improves as AI adoption increases. 
•	Employees working around 42–43 hours show peak satisfaction which means AI has not noticeably improved employee happiness.
•	No single industry dominates automation risk.
•	Technical industries require more employee upskilling.
•	There is almost nearly equal proportion of remote and on-site workers.
